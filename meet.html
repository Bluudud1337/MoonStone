A little room for everyone

Meet face to face.

Start a video call, invite people with a room name, share your screen, and chat while you talk.

Choose a room name. Everyone must use the same name to meet in the same room.
Camera and microphoneAllow browser permissions when prompted. You can mute or turn your camera off during the call.
Share your screenUse the screen-share control in the meeting toolbar. Your browser may ask you to choose a window or tab.
Chat and participantsOpen the meeting chat to send messages, or view the participant list from the call controls.

Calls are powered by Daily. Anyone with a public room name may be able to join, so avoid using sensitive names or sharing room names publicly. Camera and microphone access are requested when you join the call. Rooms expire after 24 hours.
